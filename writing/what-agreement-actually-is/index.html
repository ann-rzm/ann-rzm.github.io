“Agreeing on views” means two independently operating cognitive systems reaching, briefly and selectively, a consensus on some intersection. That premise means one hundred percent agreement is impossible.

Agreement is partial. To consolidate this local collusion, a person can temporarily tolerate or ignore enormous contradictions between the other system and their own.

Misunderstanding — not being received, or being misread — is also one purpose of expression. The majority with whom dialogue is impossible gets filtered out in the fastest way.

Difference is a rational response to different survival costs under the same structure.
