4:T5d8,This case focuses on the problem of demand translation exposed in the process of smart education moving from “resource accessibility” to “effective learning support.” Taking county junior high school mathematics students with learning difficulties as the observation scenario, the article points out that the failure of grassroots remedial support is not only due to insufficient resources, but also because students’ real learning needs often exist in implicit forms such as low scores, wrong answers, silence, and repeated failure, and are difficult to automatically transform into public service tasks that schools can identify, teachers can judge, platforms can match, and processes can verify. Around five types of breaks—the resource supply chain, the diagnostic responsibility chain, the remedial support undertaking chain, the feedback verification chain, and the data governance chain—this paper proposes a “learning breakpoint work-order system”: memory-based AI generates preliminary breakpoint judgments based on daily homework and tests; after teacher verification, these enter short in-school remedial support; and then a public closed loop is formed through verification with similar problems and a data exit mechanism. The core is not to increase digital resources, but to translate vague learning difficulties into executable remedial support tasks, preventing smart education from sliding toward resource stacking, commercial profiling, and refined involution.5:T4a9,It is not that teachers do not know that students with learning difficulties need remedial support; the problem lies in the inherent “one-to-many” organizational form of schools. It is difficult for teachers to further break down “this student is weak” into “where exactly is he stuck—which knowledge point, problem type, or cognitive step.” Teachers must daily prepare lessons, teach classes, keep up with progress, and also deal with a large amount of non-teaching affairs. The Ministry of Education’s 2023 statistical bulletin shows that the junior high school student-teacher ratio is 12.84:1, and there are still 6,184 classes with more than 56 students, including large and extra-large classes. Limited by class size and student-teacher ratio, teachers can extract common class-wide errors through regular teaching, but cannot lock onto each student’s micro-level error causes repeatedly exposed at different times and in different problem types over the long term, one by one and at a fine-grained level. If grassroots remedial support always relies on teachers’ manual judgment and recording, a systemic structural task is turned into a heavy personal labor burden.6:T4fb,According to the evolutionary logic of business, AI education products can easily deviate toward membership payments, score-improvement marketing, anxiety-inducing parent reports, mechanical drilling, and explicit ranking comparisons. Hu Xiaoyong et al. (2025) point out that artificial intelligence integrated into basic education has dual effects: it may promote new-quality educational development by reconstructing learning spaces and innovating teaching methods, but it may also fuel involution along four paths—expanding the communication field of involution (personalized recommendations precisely pushing anxious content to students), solidifying imbalanced resource allocation (AI dividends concentrated in developed regions), giving rise to evaluation alienation (intelligent evaluation tools driving everyone to pursue a standardized “ideal model”), and deepening the substitution effect (fear of being replaced by technology forcing people to learn programming at increasingly younger ages) [5].
