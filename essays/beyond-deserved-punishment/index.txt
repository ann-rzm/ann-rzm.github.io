4:T4c1,To summarize, on the one hand, let the law become a belief and interest balancer for different collectives to ensure public recognition; on the other hand, transcend deserved punishment within limits, directing towards prevention and restoration to maximize social benefits within reasonable limits. From the legislative level, more clearly incorporate purposes of transformation, restoration, and compensation in the criminal law and sentencing guidelines, and prioritize non-custodial sentences or lighter sentences when specific conditions are met (such as degree of guilt, recidivism risk assessment, victim’s willingness). From the judicial level, provide judges with greater discretion to consider individual situations and restoration possibilities. Promote restorative justice procedures. From the execution level, ensure basic human rights and humane treatment for inmates. Invest heavily in high-quality education, vocational training, psychological counseling, and other reform projects. Improve the community correction system, making it more focused on reform effectiveness rather than simple incarceration time.
